Centralised source of truth

Establishing a central consent service as the authoritative source across Microsoft Dynamics and Azure-based data platforms.

The challenge

Multiple systems needed access to reliable consent information. Without a clearly authoritative source, data can diverge and become difficult to validate or audit.

The approach

  • Designed integration between the central consent service, Microsoft Dynamics and Azure data platforms.
  • Established clear ownership of consent information.
  • Designed information flows around a central authoritative data source.
  • Considered consistency, reliability and auditability as architectural requirements.

Outcome

A clear source of truth for consent information and improved consistency across the organisation's connected systems.
