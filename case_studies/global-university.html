Case study · Distributed web estate

Large-scale web compliance

Centralising cookie compliance across hundreds of diverse university websites and providing auditable evidence of compliance.

The challenge

A large and varied web estate created a distributed compliance challenge. Different sites, technologies and content teams needed a consistent way to manage cookie and marketing requirements.

The approach

  • Delivered a centralised cookie compliance solution across hundreds of websites.
  • Addressed US cookie and marketing requirements across a distributed estate.
  • Provided central management and auditable evidence.
  • Focused on consistency while accommodating differences between individual sites.

Outcome

More consistent, measurable and auditable compliance across a large and diverse website portfolio.
