How I work

Technology agnostic.
Outcome focused.

Understand first.

Start with the business problem, information flows and constraints before selecting technology.

Simplify where possible.

Reduce unnecessary interfaces, duplication and complexity rather than adding another layer to the estate.

Make data trustworthy.

Design for validation, reconciliation, provenance and auditability when data quality matters.

Design for change.

Prefer architectures that remain understandable and maintainable as requirements, platforms and regulations evolve.
